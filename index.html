How the vault works
I.

We dig.

Library of Congress scans, Smithsonian open access, NYPL collections, Wikimedia Commons — the dusty corners of the internet where the good stuff hides behind terrible scans.

II.

We restore.

Yellowing reduced, paper tone evened, linework sharpened — by hand, plate by plate. Every work's public-domain status verified individually and documented in a provenance file. Restored, not AI.

III.

You get the goods.

Print-ready high-resolution files, an organized index, plain-language licenses. Personal packs for your walls; commercial tiers for your clients. One payment, yours forever, no royalties.
