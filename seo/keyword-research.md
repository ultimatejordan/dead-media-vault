# Dead Media Vault — Keyword Research (2026-10-06)

Method: each keyword was validated with live search for genuine content competition (dealers, collectors, auction houses, design marketplaces) and buyer intent — not dictionary-definition traffic. Every keyword maps to a Dead Media Vault sub-brand.

## Target keywords

1. **public domain botanical prints** — Dozens of Etsy sellers move digital-download packs ($5–$30); free sources (Pictureboxblue, BHL) prove massive search volume, paid packs prove willingness to pay for curation and resolution.
2. **vintage botanical wall art** — Decorator intent: people searching this want something to frame tomorrow; Etsy wall-art sellers and print-on-demand shops compete hard on it.
3. **vintage horror illustration** — Collector and designer intent: auction coverage (Harry Clarke first editions at Christie's/Ulysses Rare Books), tattoo artists, and zine designers all hunt this imagery.
4. **vintage Halloween art** — Seasonal buyer spike every Sept–Oct plus a year-round collector market (Clapsaddle cards sell $20–$600 on WorthPoint); feeds The Dread Archive's Halloween-adjacent inventory.
5. **antique maps wall art** — Decor-buying intent: dealer buying guides (loupe test, plate marks, centerfolds) and Etsy/Ebay sellers prove people buy before they read.
6. **vintage maps prints** — Volume-adjacent variant of #5; catches "vintage" phrasing from decorators who don't say "antique"; same Primary Source pack.
7. **ukiyo-e prints** — Evergreen collector + decorator demand: auction houses (Bonhams $69k complete Yoshitoshi set, 2022) at the top end, poster buyers at the bottom.
8. **japanese woodblock prints** — Broader-volume variant of #7; catches buyers who don't know the word "ukiyo-e" yet; same Gilt & Grotesque pack.
9. **vintage paper textures** — Designer buyer intent: indieground sells a 100-texture pack, YouWorkForThem sells 112 antique-paper textures — real money changes hands for this exact asset type.
10. **public domain textures** — Broader commercial-use intent (designers, video editors, game devs need licensed-clean assets); maps to Gilt & Grotesque's texture packs.

## Content mapping

- Articles 1–4 (written, staged): botanicals (#1/#2), horror illustration (#3/#4), antique maps (#5/#6), ukiyo-e (#7/#8).
- Future articles: paper textures (#9/#10) — map to a "Restored Vintage Paper Textures" piece and a "Public Domain vs Creative Commons: what 'free' actually means for commercial use" piece.

## Notes

- Avoid head terms like "public domain" alone (Wikipedia/Institutional capture, zero buyer intent).
- Every article leads with a collector/decorator problem, not a definition — the keyword enters through the headline and H2s naturally.
- Seasonal: "vintage Halloween art" should be published early September annually; the article itself is evergreen.
