What we're pressing

Dated loop kits, straight off the shellac

Every January 1st, a new year of recordings enters the public domain — and every January, the Society presses a kit from it. Jazz bands, blues shouters, vaudeville orchestras: chopped, BPM-tagged, cleaned of the worst groove noise (but never the character), and documented so you know exactly what you're sampling.

  • WAV loops and one-shots, BPM-tagged and key-labeled where determinable
  • Both the composition and the recording verified public domain — the two clearances everyone forgets
  • A free mini-kit with every release, because producers share free kits and that's the whole marketing plan

The first kit is in the pressing plant now. Meanwhile, three of our four vaults are already open — go make something with the free plates while the wax cools.
