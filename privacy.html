Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

This website collects nothing. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no newsletter signup hiding in the footer. It's a static site — there is nowhere for your data to go.

Downloads

Free teaser packs download directly from this site. Your browser may log the download locally, as browsers do; we never see it.

Purchases

Paid packs are sold through Gumroad. When you buy something, Gumroad handles checkout, payment, and delivery under Gumroad's privacy policy. We receive order information (what you bought, your email for delivery) through Gumroad's seller dashboard and use it only to fulfill your order and fix problems.

Third parties on this site

  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces. Google may log the font request per its own policies.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site. GitHub logs standard web-server data per its privacy statement.

That's the complete list.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone, which makes this paragraph admirably easy to comply with.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tacatshi@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Given that the current data-collection total is zero, any change would be newsworthy.
